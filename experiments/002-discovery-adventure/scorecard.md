## What improved from Experiment 001

Experiment 001 exposed that the model had mistaken a recurring feature of favorite games for a stronger causal preference.

Experiment 002 directly tested the revised claim instead of simply adding more random titles. The revised H01/H07 distinction held across four targeted items.

## Calibration

Calibration improved compared with Experiment 001:
- no polarity misses;
- both errors were only one intensity step;
- the highest-confidence strong-positive prediction, Fury Road, was correct.

There is still room to improve strongest-tier calibration:
- 999 was underpredicted;
- Pirates was overpredicted.

## Experiment decision

**Keep the revised discovery model. Do not add a new adventure hypothesis yet.**

The next useful work should shift from proving static taste hypotheses toward testing the actual Tastemake product loop: recommendation selection, predicted reaction, user feedback, and hypothesis revision.
